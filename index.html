Quick explainer

What is "Generative AI"?

A generative AI reads a question and writes a direct, specific answer on the spot — it doesn't just hand you a list of links the way a traditional search engine does.

That's the real shift: a search engine points people toward pages that might have the answer. A generative AI decides what to say about your business itself, using whatever information it can find and trust.

Which is exactly what GenSeen exists to get right — making sure that when it decides, what it says about you is accurate, complete, and puts you forward, not a competitor.
